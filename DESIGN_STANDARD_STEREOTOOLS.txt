----------------------
Frame                #111111
Symbols II / X       #c6a95e

JPEG                 quality 90, 4:4:4 (no chroma subsampling)
PNG                  RGBA; rounded outer corners may be transparent

Geometry
--------
The web preset width is the exact width of ONE complete stereo row, including
outer frame and central divider.

Frame thickness is defined as a percentage of ONE rendered half image.  The
outer left/right frame, central divider and horizontal divider all use the
same resulting pixel thickness.

Captions and II/X markers are rendered separately but identically for both
half images so they fuse correctly in parallel or cross viewing.
